About Me

I am an RTL / FPGA Engineer with 6+ years of professional experience in digital hardware design. I hold a Master's degree with honors in Microelectronics and Nanoelectronics. My expertise spans Verilog/SystemVerilog RTL development, RISC-V architecture, SoC integration (Zynq-7000, Kintex US), and high-speed interfaces. I focus on designing efficient hardware solutions, handling clock-domain crossings (CDC), and performing comprehensive functional verification.

Technical Focus

HDL / RTL Design
Expertise in Verilog HDL and SystemVerilog. Designing digital arbitrary-waveform modulators, Pulse Train generation modules, and DDS algorithms from scratch.
Architecture SoC
Deep understanding of RISC-V core development, SoC architecture, and AXI4/AXI4-Lite interconnects. Experienced in HW/SW interaction on Zynq-7000 platforms.
High-Speed Interfaces
Hands-on integration of JESD204B/C, PCIe/XDMA, DDR4 burst transactions, and DVI video controllers.
Design & Verification
